# BASTION Product Plan

## Thesis

Treat defense as a mission-readiness and delivery network, not an account list
or equipment inventory. Measure organization, personnel, availability,
maintenance, acquisition, suppliers, logistics, interoperability, resilience,
lifecycle cost, transition risk, and auditability together.

## Planned product shape

- Public, aggregate, unclassified, source-labelled corpus.
- Non-operational mission/readiness, sustainment, acquisition, and supplier models.
- Versioned promises, gaps, null results, stress tests, constrained alternatives,
  economics, delivery evidence, and held Taxlane handoffs.
- Deterministic Rust artifacts only after accepted work packages.

## Current boundary

The accepted empty Rust workspace contains no product semantics. Review and
isolated-test scaffolds are acceptance-ready; semantic implementation remains
gated. No force plan, operational recommendation, official request, savings
estimate, Taxlane admission, allocation, rate change, or release.
