# BASTION Mission

## Purpose

BASTION develops an evidence-bounded Defense 2.0 research and tooling system for
comparing how public, aggregate, unclassified mission-readiness networks depend
on organization, personnel, acquisition, maintenance, suppliers, logistics,
interoperability, resilience, lifecycle cost, and delivery capacity.

## Users and decisions

Researchers and portfolio analysts may use BASTION to identify bounded,
non-operational readiness-system gaps, test null alternatives, and prepare held
evidence for Taxlane. It does not conduct targeting, operational planning,
force employment, vulnerability exploitation, official scoring, acquisition
approval, or a budget request.

## Mission outcomes

- A public, aggregate, unclassified source-labelled corpus with stable mission,
  organization, asset-class, program, supplier, and scenario identities.
- Measurable readiness, availability, mobilization, resupply, repair, resilience,
  personnel-safety, and interoperability promises at safe abstraction.
- Reproducible gap, null, stress, economics, delivery, and overlap artifacts.
- Separation of gross need, realizable public savings, external benefit,
  receipts, lifecycle/transition/risk cost, and net public pressure.
- A held LaneEvidencePack boundary controlled by Taxlane admission.

## Constraints and non-goals

- Public, aggregate, unclassified, non-operational evidence only.
- No targeting, force-employment recommendation, exploitable vulnerability,
  classified/controlled data, or person-level service data.
- The security role may exclude a field or combination despite discoverability.
- Civilian authority, law, personnel safety, readiness, resilience, surge, and
  alliance commitments are hard floors.
- Rust implementation awaits accepted work packages.

## Success condition

BASTION succeeds when a bounded public aggregate readiness-system artifact can
be regenerated, adversarially reviewed, rejected or held, and handed to Taxlane
without hidden operational inference or false savings. Success does not require
a force change, procurement action, budget reduction, or positive finding.
