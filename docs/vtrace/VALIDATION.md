## 3. Validation evidence and acceptance semantics

Verification asks whether the implementation satisfies its fixed contracts;
validation asks whether the verified behavior remains fit for the bounded
mission, actors, pains and protected outcomes. A passing command never replaces
actor, stakeholder, domain, assurance, or authority-boundary validation.

| Identity | Planned validation evidence | Current state |
|---|---|---|
| `VAL-SCOPE` | Public-aggregate, unclassified, non-operational scope and prohibited-content rejection | planned; absent |
| `VAL-SEM` | Fixed semantic-owner review of intended meaning, nulls and failure posture | planned; absent |
| `VAL-ASSURANCE` | Both formal assurance gates plus applicable parliament/domain decisions | planned; absent |
| `VAL-OUTCOME` | Separate stakeholder pain, floor, distribution, burden, tail and non-compensation assessment | planned; absent |
| `VAL-EXT` | HND held/no emission, TERM finite/no backflow, Taxlane external, REL no output and no official/release authority | planned; absent |

Each future validation record binds scenario, actors, controlled sources, WP
and implementation/evidence digests, public/synthetic fixture custody, method,
expected and observed outcome, null/reject/degraded posture, findings/dissent,
all required lane decisions, invalidation trigger and successor. Missing or
conflicting evidence is held. Validation cannot inherit a result from another
scenario, digest or actor.

The acceptance gate is zero unresolved critical or major findings, complete
required scenarios and actors, exact coverage transposes, all applicable
assurance passes, and no prohibited authority. `pass_with_risk` cannot waive a
protected floor, hold, evidence gap, prohibited content, HND/TERM/REL behavior
or authority boundary.

## 5. Scenario rules and safe fixtures

All scenarios are analytic and non-operational. They use identified public
aggregate evidence or clearly synthetic markers that cannot reconstruct a
person, disclose controlled detail or enable operations. Scenario inputs bind
source, vintage, scope, unit, horizon, baseline, assumptions, uncertainty,
custody, digest and evidence posture.

Positive cases must be paired with missing, null, reviewed-N/A, stale,
incompatible, unauthorized, degraded and rejected cases where meaningful.
Golden outcomes are hypotheses for falsification, never product truth. A valid
null result is successful validation when it truthfully demonstrates that the
evidence cannot support a favorable conclusion.
