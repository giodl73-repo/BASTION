# BASTION Role Index

All promotable planning, corpus, readiness, gap, candidate, economics, delivery,
and handoff artifacts receive `pass`, `finding`, or `defer`. Parliament is
adversarial and no voice is skipped. Editorial review follows substantive
review. Both assurance roles must pass any safety-sensitive promotion.

| File | Voice | Primary tension |

| `parliament/civilian-strategy-force-planner.md` | Civilian Strategy & Force Planner | Public missions and civilian priorities vs. inherited structure |

## Panel review

`panel-reviewer/panel.md` defines methodology archetypes. It confers no external
approval and may not solicit or simulate classified expertise.

## Fixed point

A stage advances only when no critical or major actionable finding remains,
every defer names a later artifact or work package, dissent is preserved, both
assurance gates pass, and the active wave records the disposition.
