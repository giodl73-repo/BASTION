# BASTION House Rules

1. Use public, aggregate, unclassified evidence only.
2. No targeting, operational planning, exploitable vulnerability, classified or
   controlled data, or person-level service records.
3. Model missions and readiness flows, never infer readiness from spending or
   inventory alone.
4. Civilian control, law, personnel safety, readiness, resilience, surge, and
   alliance obligations are hard floors.
5. Savings include transition, lifecycle, supplier, workforce, and risk costs;
   burden shifts and readiness degradation are not efficiencies.
6. Missing evidence is held/null, never zero. A null result is valid.
7. The classification/security role may reject a field even if discoverable.
8. Advance VTRACE one deliverable at a time through `.roles` fixed point.
9. No implementation before an accepted work package.
10. Child work precedes any TRACKER pointer update; no release is implied.
